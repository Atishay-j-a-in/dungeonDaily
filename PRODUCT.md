# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo fitness adventurers playing on their phone mid-workout: glanceable at arm's length, gym lighting, sweaty hands. The job to be done is finishing a 10–30 minute workout disguised as a voice-driven fantasy adventure.

## Product Purpose

DungeonsDaily turns short physical workouts into immersive fantasy adventures. Gemma acts as Dungeon Master and a deterministic game engine stays authoritative over rules and state, served synchronously through Express. Success looks like a player finishing the workout because their character needed to survive, forgetting it was exercise.

## Brand Personality

Ember dungeon: dark green-black stone surfaces lit with vibrant, bright
green, molten gold reserved for the moments that matter. Mysterious,
dramatic, encouraging. A Dungeon Master guiding one adventurer through a
dangerous world — never a fitness instructor, never a generic chatbot.

## Anti-references

- Generic fitness apps: sporty dashboards, progress rings, charts, neon energy colors.
- Neon arcade game aesthetics: glowing cyber palettes, gamified clutter.
- AI-slop scaffolding: eyebrow kickers on every section, gradient text, identical icon-card grids, numbered section markers.

## Design Principles

1. Glanceable mid-rep: big touch targets, high contrast, one primary action per screen.
2. Voice first, screen second: the orb and narration carry the scene; the UI never demands reading mid-set.
3. Luxury restraint: deep green-black surfaces with vibrant green lift, gold spent sparingly on DM speech, exercise calls, and victory.
4. Motion with mercy: vestibular-safe defaults, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored, plus an explicit orb-stillness mode.
5. Decorative audio never blocks play: every sound has a fallback and a mute.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: body text ≥4.5:1, touch targets ≥44px, visible focus, live-region announcements for turns. Extra motion care for vestibular sensitivity (stillness toggle, no vestibular triggers by default).
